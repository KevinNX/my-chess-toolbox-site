My Chess Toolbox

A personal chess training app for Android and Windows.

What it does

  • Train on puzzle collections imported from PGN files.
  • Drill opening repertoires.
  • Keep and annotate your own games.
  • Read a library of PGN books, with a search across files and folders.
  • Analyse positions with a chess engine running on your device.
  • Tools: statistics, an analysis board and a chess clock.

Your data stays yours

Everything is stored in a folder you choose: on your device, or in your own Google Drive. On Android, you can connect that folder through your Google account so that changes made on your PC are seen right away. The app only reads and writes the database folder you choose; it has no server and collects nothing. See the privacy policy.

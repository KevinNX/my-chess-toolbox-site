Privacy policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Summary

My Chess Toolbox collects no personal data. It has no server, no account of its own, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Your chess data stays in a folder you choose.

Where your data is stored

The app keeps its database (puzzle collections, openings, your games, your PGN library, training history and statistics) as ordinary files in a folder you pick: on your device, in a storage app, or in your own Google Drive. A few settings (such as the language or where you stopped reading) are kept on the device only. Nothing is sent to the developer or to any third party.

Google Drive access (Android)

If you choose to connect through your Google account, the app asks for permission to access your Google Drive (scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive). It uses this permission only to:

  • show your Drive folders so you can choose the database folder;
  • read and write the files inside the folder you chose (for example, games you add on your PC and games you edit on your phone).

The app does not read, change or delete any other file in your Drive, does not share your files, and does not send Drive data anywhere other than between your device and your own Drive. Access tokens stay on your device. A deleted file is moved to the Drive trash, not erased.

The app’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

You can remove the app’s access at any time from your Google Account, under Third-party apps & services.

Chess engine

Positions are analysed by an engine running on your device. No position or game is sent online.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
